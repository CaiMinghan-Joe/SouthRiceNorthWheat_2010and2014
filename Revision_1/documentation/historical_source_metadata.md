# Historical agricultural ecology source

- Article: Guntuku et al. (2024), *Historical patterns of rice farming explain modern-day language use in China and Japan more than modernization and urbanization*, Humanities and Social Sciences Communications.
- Article URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-024-04053-7
- OSF project: https://osf.io/mg39f/?view_only=2b6ec72006844ef3aaeafb37255b38c6
- Downloaded file: `Talhelm China Province-Level Indicators - Weibo Open Data.xlsx` (OSF GUID `4bc25`).
- Local filename: `Talhelm_China_Province_Indicators.xlsx`.
- SHA-256: `b74f591aa30b2db0223cd190bad1679c89511ef28613c134a536c1799f3a3d0c`.
- Historical rice measure: `PercentPaddy`, the proportion of cultivated land devoted to paddy rice; province values are from the 1996 China Statistical Yearbook according to the article.
- Validation reported by source article: province-level 1996 measure correlates with available 1914 rice data at r=0.95 for 22 provinces.
- Additional regional controls used here: `PercapitaGDP1996` and `PercentUrbanPopulation2016`.
- Geographic resolution: province. CFPS public-use `provcd` permits a province merge; prefecture/county matching would require restricted CFPS geography and a separate access process.
- Interpretation boundary: historical paddy share is an exposure/proxy, not an instrument. It reduces dependence on current household crop choice but does not establish exogeneity.
